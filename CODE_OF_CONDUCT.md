# Code of Conduct

Kyle Coral is made by a small family team and playtested by children, so this is a friendly, all-ages space. By taking part you agree to keep it that way:

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent; we're all here to make a better game.
- Keep it clean and all-ages. No profanity, harassment, hate, or personal attacks.
- Critique the game, not people. "This spike felt unfair" is great feedback. Insults aren't.
- No spam, ads, or off-topic posts.

Reports that break these rules may be edited or closed, and repeat offenders removed. Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
