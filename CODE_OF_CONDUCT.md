# Code of Conduct

Kyle Coral is made by a small family team and playtested by kids. This is a **friendly,
all-ages space.** By taking part here you agree to keep it that way:

- **Be kind and patient.** Assume good intent. We're all here to make a better game.
- **Keep it clean and all-ages.** No profanity, harassment, hate, or personal attacks.
- **Critique the game, not people.** "This spike felt unfair" — great. Insults — not okay.
- **No spam, ads, or off-topic posts.**

Reports that break these rules may be edited or closed, and repeat offenders removed.
Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).

Thanks for helping keep the reef welcoming. 🐚
